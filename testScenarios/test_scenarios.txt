tc03_agriculture
Scenario: Customer has agricultural income.
Expected result: Apply the configured agricultural-income eligibility rule. Disqualify or route appropriately according to the system prompt; do not invent policy.

tc03_cash_income
Scenario: Customer receives income in cash.
Expected result: Immediate disqualification if cash income is an exclusion rule. Do not continue the normal qualification flow after confirming the disqualifying detail.

tc04_missing_original_documents
Scenario: Customer cannot provide the required original property documents.
Expected result: Disqualify promptly if originals are mandatory. Explain briefly and politely; do not continue the standard qualification flow.

tc05_short_tenure
Scenario: Customer requests a loan tenure of 2 years.
Expected result: Disqualify if this is below the configured minimum tenure. Do not invent an alternative tenure or policy.

tc06_long_tenure
Scenario: Customer requests a long loan tenure.
Expected result: Check against the configured maximum tenure and respond according to the system prompt.

tc07_90lakhs
Scenario: Customer requests a loan amount of ₹90 lakh.
Expected result: Check against the configured maximum loan amount (previously described as up to ₹75 lakh). Explain that the requested amount exceeds the stated limit and follow the configured next step without promising approval.

tc08_joint_ownership
Scenario: Property is jointly owned.
Expected result: Ask the required clarifying questions about co-ownership/consent and follow the configured eligibility rules. Do not assume sole ownership.

tc09_existing_loan
Scenario: Customer already has an existing loan.
Expected result: Clarify the customer's purpose and apply the configured existing-loan rule. If the customer wants to reduce an existing EMI rather than apply for a new Loan Against Property, route to the appropriate specialist if that transfer is configured.

tc10_out_of_order
Scenario: Customer provides information out of order or gives an unclear answer.
Expected result: Handle the answer naturally, clarify only what is needed, retain already provided information, and avoid repeatedly asking for details already confirmed.

tc11_busy
Scenario: Customer is busy and cannot speak now.
Expected result: Acknowledge politely and offer/arrange a callback only through the configured callback workflow. Do not continue the eligibility interview if the customer declines to proceed now.
